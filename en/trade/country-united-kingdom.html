In the 12 months to Oct 2026, for every 100 full containers Korea shipped to the United Kingdom, 61 full containers came back; 31% of the boxes arriving from the United Kingdom were empty. More full boxes leave Korea for the United Kingdom than come back, so ships heading to Korea usually have spare room.

Sources: Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries container statistics (TEU, by partner country); Korea Customs Service average freight on import and export declarations.
