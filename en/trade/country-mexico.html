Korea–Mexico trade

In the 12 months to October 2026, Korea sold $15.5bn of goods to Mexico and bought $9.80bn. Mexico took 1.5% of all Korean exports.

From October 2025 to September 2026, 72% of containers arriving in Korea from Mexico were empty (Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries); for every 100 loaded boxes Korea sent to Mexico, about eight came back loaded.

In the 12 months to Sep 2026, for every 100 full containers Korea shipped to Mexico, 8 full containers came back; 72% of the boxes arriving from Mexico were empty. More full boxes leave Korea for Mexico than come back, so ships heading to Korea usually have spare room.

Sources: Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries container statistics (TEU, by partner country); Korea Customs Service average freight on import and export declarations.
