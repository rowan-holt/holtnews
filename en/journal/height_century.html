Korean women grew taller than any other women in the world over 100 years

A century of trends in adult human height
NCD Risk Factor Collaboration · eLife, 2016
2026.10.21

01Abstract

Over 100 years, Korean women grew taller by 20.2 cm, the biggest gain for women in the world. Korean men grew 15.2 cm taller.

02Method

Researchers used 1,472 studies with over 18.6 million people in 200 countries. They used only measured heights, not heights people reported themselves.

03Findings

Korean men and women are now taller than Japanese men and women. Growth for Korean men started to level off with those born in the 1980s. Dutch men born in 1996 are the tallest, at 182.5 cm on average.

04Limitations

It compares people born in 1896 and 1996 at age 18. It does not follow the same people for 100 years.

Original paper
