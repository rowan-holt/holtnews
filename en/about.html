About HOLT NEWS

HOLT NEWS explains viral research and world stories in plain words. We publish in Korean and English.

Research

We pick studies in psychology, health and society that people want to share. We don't rely on news coverage alone: we read the original paper and the university or journal releases, and we list what a study does not show under "Limitations".
