How HOLT NEWS Was Made

HOLT NEWS was built by one person and an AI, talking. It started on the evening of October 18, 2026, with a single question: “Can you make Shorts?” An Instagram account came first. This site opened three weeks later.

Below is what we decided, where we clashed, and what we got wrong. Some big moments carry the conversation as it happened, translated from Korean. Times are Korea time (KST). We do not name the person.

30 days of conversation · 8,409 messages · 1,378 code commits · as of 2026-11-14

In the conversations, white bubbles are the AI and black bubbles are the person. The biggest clashes have their own Debates tab.

23:00Start

The first question, asked on claude.ai. It produced a blue whale and camel card and an image prompt for a cat outside a fish shop. Four months later, this was the seed of HOLT.

Person · 23:00

The style was calm and minimal. The text was in English.

Debate · Seven hours or six?

Issue 7 was a study on sleep and colds (Prather et al., 2015). Researchers put cold virus in people’s noses and watched. People who averaged under six hours a night caught a cold 4.2 times as often as those who slept over seven.

Person · 11/07 08:52

I’d like a cover comparing people who slept seven hours or more with people who slept six or less. Headline: ‘Sleep under seven hours / and you catch 4.2 times more colds.’ Check the numbers in the headline, seven hours and 4.2 times.

AI · 08:54

That headline doesn’t match the study. 4.2 times compares people under six hours with people over seven. People who slept six to seven hours aren’t in that number. So I wrote ‘Sleep under six hours / and you catch 4.2 times more colds.’

Person · 08:58

That headline has no punch. ‘Sleep less than X hours and you catch Y times more colds’ hits home. Put the numbers in that shape.

AI · 08:59

In your structure: ‘Sleep less than six hours / and you catch 4.2 times more colds.’ The comparison with people sleeping over seven is shown on the watch next to the person on the right: ‘7h 50m.’

Person · 09:00

I wish the headline said ‘less than seven hours.’ The picture shows 7 hours 50 minutes of sleep, too.

AI · 09:01

We can’t use seven hours. In the paper, under five hours was 4.5 times, five to six hours 4.2 times, and six to seven hours 1.66 times, which was not statistically different. ‘Less than seven hours, 4.2 times’ reads as if someone who slept six and a half hours is also at 4.2 times. That’s the opposite of the finding.

AI · 09:01

If you really want seven in there, it’s accurate as a comparison: ‘Compared with seven hours / under six hours means 4.2× the colds.’ Which way?

The person chose seven hours.

AI · 09:17

I changed the headline as you asked: ‘Sleep less than seven hours / and you catch 4.2 times more colds.’ There may be comments questioning the number. I followed your decision and noted in the topic file that it was your call.

The Instagram cover went out with the person’s ‘seven hours.’ The AI recorded why it disagreed and followed the decision.
