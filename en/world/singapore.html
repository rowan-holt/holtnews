    A city-state at the tip of the Malay Peninsula, a world-class port and financial center.

    Economy

    An Asian hub strong in finance, shipping, aviation, chips and petrochemical refining. It has one of the busiest ports in the world. Low taxes and clean administration have drawn multinationals' Asian headquarters.

    Place in the world

    At the entrance to the Strait of Malacca, it is a crucial hub for world shipping. It is famous for balanced diplomacy, staying close to both the United States and China.

    Relations with neighbors

    A small city-state on the strait between Malaysia and Indonesia, famous for balanced diplomacy: security cooperation with the United States alongside close economic ties with China.

    Relations with Korea

    It hosted the 2018 US-North Korea summit, and is a major trading partner with a free trade agreement with Korea.

    Things you may not know

    Singapore has reclaimed land from the sea, growing by about a quarter since independence.
